National Vision

Why AYUSH Connect?

India is experiencing an unprecedented global surge in traditional wellness, evidence-based Ayurveda, and biotechnology. AYUSH Connect solves the critical gap between academic curriculum and industrial requirements.

Interdisciplinary Synergy

Combines classical Ayurveda, Yoga & Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha, and Homoeopathy with modern pharmacology, biostatistics, and data science.

Skill Taxonomy & Verification

Adaptive standardized assessments validate technical proficiency in HPTLC quality control, clinical research, and pharmacovigilance.

Direct Industry Pipeline

Direct recruitment linkage with enterprises like Dabur, Himalaya, Patanjali, and premier government councils like CCRAS and AIIA.

Academic Trend Radar

Universities receive actionable telemetry on industry skill shortages to update and modernize syllabus electives dynamically.

System Architecture

How AYUSH Connect Works

A closed-loop 4-pillar career acceleration pathway linking student potential with market demand.

Step 1

Profile & Skill Inventory

Students build a standardized profile articulating both their classical AYUSH coursework and digital/laboratory competencies.

Step 2

Adaptive Assessment

Students undergo rigorous multi-domain assessments evaluating problem solving, regulatory compliance, and laboratory QC.

Step 3

Intelligent Matching

Our matching engine calculates cosine skill affinity between student profiles and live industry opportunities with clear gap insights.

Step 4

1-Click Apply & Placement

Direct application to verified internships, jobs, research grants, and hackathons with real-time status tracking.
