1. Overview & Scope

AYUSH CONNECT ("Platform", "we", "us", or "our") is the national career, research, and skill ecosystem initiated to bridge scholars of Ayurveda, Yoga, Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha, and Homoeopathy with validated clinical healthcare industry partners, research institutions, and academic faculty.

This Privacy Policy articulates our transparent commitments regarding the collection, handling, storage, and processing of personal data for all enrolled Students, verified Academic Faculty, and accredited Industry Entities. By logging into or creating an account on AYUSH CONNECT, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

Key Guarantee: AYUSH CONNECT does not sell, lease, or monetize individual student resumes or demographic data to commercial marketing agencies or ad brokers. Data is processed exclusively to verify competence, provide AI skill recommendations, and match clinical and research placements.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information directly provided by users upon account onboarding, educational assessments, and clinical verification:

Student & Scholar Data
  • Profile Information: Full name, institutional email address, telephone contact, profile image avatar.
  • Academic Records: AYUSH discipline (e.g. BAMS, BHMS, BNYS, MD/MS), institution name, year of study, state council registration.
  • Skill Assessments: Clinical competencies, botanical pharmacognosy skills, NAMASTE / ICD-11 coding proficiencies.
  • Applications: Submitted portfolios, internship cover notes, research abstracts.
Industry & Faculty Data
  • Organization Details: Corporate entity name, CIN / GSTIN registration, manufacturing drug licensing authority.
  • Authorized Representative: Designation, corporate email domain, contact details.
  • Opportunity Listings: Job descriptions, stipend packages, clinical trial research scopes, mentorship criteria.
  • Academic Credentialing: University department, publications, research chair verification.

Automated Telemetry: For platform security, audit trails, and anti-fraud verification, we record IP addresses, browser user-agent tokens, login session timestamps, and administrative audit actions in our Supabase Cloud infrastructure.

3. How We Use Your Data

The information gathered on AYUSH CONNECT is employed strictly for verifiable educational and professional outcomes:

Purpose of Processing Categories of Data Lawful Basis
Skill Assessment & AI Recommendations AYUSH subject scores, completed quizzes, clinical competencies User Consent & Educational Purpose
Internship & Job Applications Student resume, contact details, project portfolios Direct Performance of Opportunity Contract
Industry Admin Approval & Screening Corporate CIN, GSTIN, faculty department verification Platform Integrity & Statutory Due Diligence
Institutional Skill Analytics Aggregated, anonymized competence benchmarks across colleges Public Educational Research & National Skill Mapping

4. Google OAuth & Federated Identity

AYUSH CONNECT provides single-click authentication via Google Identity Services (OAuth 2.0) for verified students, scholars, and institutional users.

Scopes Requested & Stored
  • openid: Cryptographically verifies your authenticated identity.
  • email: Captures your institutional or personal Gmail address for dashboard notifications and session management.
  • profile: Reads your display name and public avatar to populate your student dashboard card.
What We NEVER Access
  • We do not access your private Gmail inbox, emails, or drafts.
  • We do not access your Google Drive documents or photos.
  • We do not request or store your Google account password.
  • Authentication tokens are securely exchanged directly via Supabase Auth with TLS encryption.

You may revoke AYUSH CONNECT's access to your Google account at any time by visiting Google Account Security Permissions.

5. Data Sharing & Disclosure

AYUSH CONNECT exercises rigorous constraints on disclosing any user information:

  • To Registered Recruiters & Employers: When a student applies for an opportunity, their candidate profile, verified skills, portfolio attachments, and contact information are made accessible to the prospective employer.
  • To Affiliated Universities & Colleges: Institutional department heads have access to aggregate internship placement rates and verified assessment milestones of their enrolled students.
  • To Government Regulatory Bodies: Data may be disclosed only when legally mandated by judicial orders, the Ministry of AYUSH, or statutory regulatory frameworks under Indian law.

6. Security Architecture & Encryption

All data within AYUSH CONNECT is protected by modern cloud security best practices:

Transmission & Rest Security
  • Transport Security: 256-bit TLS 1.3 encryption across all client-to-cloud communications.
  • At-Rest Encryption: AES-256 database disk encryption on Supabase PostgreSQL storage nodes.
  • Row-Level Security (RLS): Granular database policies ensure students can only view or modify their personal records.
Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
  • Strict Role Partitioning: Students, Industry Partners, Academicians, and Government Admins are isolated at database level.
  • Admin Verification: Industry entities and academicians must be authenticated before viewing student candidate pools.

7. Data Retention & User Rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you retain definitive ownership and control over your personal data:

  • Right to Access & Portability: You may review, download, or export your complete skill profile and application history from your dashboard settings at any time.
  • Right to Rectification: You may update inaccuracies in your academic scores, contact information, or portfolio attachments directly in real-time.
  • Right to Erasure (Forget Me): Upon graduating or leaving the platform, you can request full permanent deletion of your profile, assessments, and application submissions.
